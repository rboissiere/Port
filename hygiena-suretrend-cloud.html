Founding Marketing & Product Hire

From One-Time Visitors to Active Users

For years, Hygiena's software sat in the background, there mainly to check a box. Customers hardly used it, if at all, and Hygiena was missing a huge chance to stand out. Using insights from user research, I launched Hygiena's first subscription software, defining both what was built and how its story was told. It changed how customers related to Hygiena, from a purchase once a year to a tool they used every day.

My Approach

  1. Studied How People Really Used It

    I found three distinct needs: collecting data on the floor, reviewing it at the computer, and making decisions from it, often in another building or state. The old software served one of them at best, and sometimes none.

  2. Ran Alpha and Beta Trials

    I invited customers to try early versions. Their feedback shaped what launched, and many became its first champions.

  3. Gave People a Reason to Come Back

    I made the case for features no earlier option or competitor offered. Results synced from the handheld over Wi-Fi. Heat maps on a facility floor plan showed problem spots at a glance. Reports arrived by email wherever people were, and alerts flagged a failed test right away. For the first time, everyone from the floor to leadership saw the same picture, every day.

  4. Packaged It for Everyone

    I designed flexible subscription options, from a single user to global organizations, so every customer had a clear way in and value to match.

  5. Told Customers' Stories

    I turned trial customers' results into case studies, so prospects heard from peers, not just from Hygiena.

  6. Created an Ecosystem

    I designed it to work with the flagship EnSURE Touch and with every product to come, from new launches to acquired companies. One shared home for all their results made cross-selling natural.
